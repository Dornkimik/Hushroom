A note on privacy

You’re given a random alias and a temporary session cookie. No account, email address, or real name is requested.

Public rooms are visible to all visitors. Private chats are available only to their two participants through this app. They are not end-to-end encrypted; the server processes their contents.

Only the latest 100 messages per conversation are kept in server memory. They disappear when the server restarts. Inactive identities and their private history expire after 24 hours. Room names and descriptions are saved to disk.

Your hosting provider may keep connection logs, including IP addresses. Anonymous here means no public identity, not untraceable activity.
