A QUIETER PLACE TO TALK

Anonymous chat with no registration

Open SilenzaChat, get a random alias, and start talking. You do not need to create an account, provide an email address, or use your real name. Join a public room, choose someone online for a private conversation, or make a temporary room for a group.

Choose the room that fits

Public chat rooms

Talk with anyone in an open room. Public messages can be read by other visitors and the server. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

Encrypted private chat

Choose someone online to start a one-to-one chat. Your browser encrypts messages and images before sending them; the other person’s browser decrypts them.

Encrypted temporary rooms

Create an open or invite-only room for up to 20 members. Room messages and images are end-to-end encrypted for current members. Rooms expire after 24 hours without activity or on server restart.

How to start an anonymous chat

  1. Open the chat. SilenzaChat gives your browser a random alias automatically.
  2. Pick a conversation. Enter a public room, select someone online for a private chat, or create a temporary room.
  3. Check before sharing. Public rooms are visible to others. For private chats, check the encryption status and compare verification codes through another trusted channel if you need to confirm a contact’s key.

What “anonymous” and “encrypted” mean here

No registration means other visitors see a random alias instead of a name you provide. It does not make you untraceable: the server handles your IP address, and your host or network provider may keep logs. What you write or show in an image can identify you.

Private chats and temporary rooms protect message contents with end-to-end encryption. The server can still see metadata such as aliases, room membership, message times, and encrypted data sizes. The website code delivered to your browser and your device also matter for security. The app has not had an independent security audit.

Recent chat history is held temporarily in server memory. A server restart clears it; private sessions expire after 24 hours offline. SilenzaChat is for conversations, not permanent storage.
